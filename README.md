## 🧠 About Me

I'm **Dwijesh**. I build **backend systems**, experiment with **applied ML**, and take interesting problems a little further than I probably need to. Lately, that habit is pointed at **LLM agents**.

- 📅 At **Nusmark** (AI calendar assistant), I built much of the **Google & Outlook calendar sync** and the tools our **LLM agents** used to find meeting times. Getting humans to agree on a time wasn't hard enough, so we added AI.
- 🔔 Built much of the reminder service: **58,000 of 60,500 delivered (96%)**, plus 55,000 cancelled as plans changed. Sending a notification is easy; knowing when *not* to is the hard part.
- 🫀 Trained a **dual-stream CNN** to read heart rate from facial video (**r = 0.87** on UBFC-rPPG) for my First-Class dissertation at Southampton.
- 🤖 Now building **LLM agent pipelines** with browser automation, aiming for useful over demo-impressive.
- 🍓 Away from the keyboard: at a different keyboard, tinkering with a small fleet of **Raspberry Pis**.
